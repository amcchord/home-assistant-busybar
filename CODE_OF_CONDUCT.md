# Code of conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone. Be kind, assume good intent, critique ideas rather than people, and respect differences in experience and perspective.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information, and sustained disruption. Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban participants whose behavior is harmful.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical.
